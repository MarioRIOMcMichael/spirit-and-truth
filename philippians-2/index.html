The guide

Each step is marked so you know what it's asking of you.

ReadDiscussReflectPray

  1. Open in prayer · 6:30

    Pray

    Ask God to settle our minds and show us where we've put ourselves first.

  2. Lead-in: Luke 18:14 · 6:35

    ReadReflect

    Last time we watched two men pray. Jesus told us who went home justified. Paul now shows us what that low place looks like in daily life.

    14I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other: for every one that exalteth himself shall be abased; and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted.Luke 18:14, KJV ↗ · NIV ↗
    • Since Luke 18, where have you caught yourself keeping score?

  3. Read the passage out loud · 6:40

    Read
    3Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves. 4Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others. 5Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus: 6Who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: 7But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men: 8And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.Philippians 2:3–8, KJV ↗ · NIV ↗

    How it's built

    1. v. 3 The command: do nothing from strife or empty glory. Count others better than yourself.
    2. v. 4 The practice: look past your own things to the things of others.
    3. v. 5 The turn: this way of thinking belongs to Christ, and it can be ours.
    4. v. 6–8 The example: Christ goes down, step by step, all the way to the cross.

  4. What Paul asks of us · 6:45

    Discuss

    Paul isn't telling us to think badly of ourselves. He's telling us where to put our attention.

    • What's the difference between thinking less of yourself and thinking of yourself less?
    • Where does vainglory show up for men at work, at home, or at church?
    • What makes verse 4 hard when you're already tired?

  5. What Jesus gave up · 7:00

    ReadDiscuss

    Walk verses 6 through 8 slowly. Count the steps down.

    • Jesus had every right and didn't hold on to it. Which of your rights is hardest to let go of?
    • How does knowing the end of the story change how you serve when nobody notices?

  6. Carry it home · 7:10

    Reflect

    Pick one person. Name one way to put their things ahead of yours this week, and do it without telling anyone.

    • Who is that person, and what will it cost you?

  7. Prayer requests and close · 7:20

    Pray

    Share what you're carrying. Pray for each other, then close.
