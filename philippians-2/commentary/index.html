The Mind of Christ

Philippians 2:3–8

Good morning, and welcome. Grab your coffee and your Bible. This week we're in Philippians chapter two.

Last time we stood in the temple with two men. One listed his record. The other asked for mercy. Jesus finished with this:

"I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other: for every one that exalteth himself shall be abased; and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted."

So what does the low place look like on a Tuesday? Paul tells us.

The Command

"Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves. Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others."

Paul isn't asking you to pretend you have no gifts. He's asking you to move your eyes. Most of us walk through the day looking at our own things. Paul says look up.

The Example

Then he gives the reason, and the reason is a person.

"Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus: Who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men: And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross."

Count the steps. Each one goes lower. He had every right, and He didn't hold on to it. He went all the way down, to the death of the cross.

Bring It Home

Pick one person this week. Put their things ahead of yours, and don't tell anyone. That's the mind Paul is talking about.

Ask, seek, knock. See you Friday.
